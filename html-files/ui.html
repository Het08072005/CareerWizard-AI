⬡ STAR Method Framework
S
Situation
Set the context — where, when, what was the challenge you faced?
T
Task
Define your responsibility — what was expected of you specifically?
A
Action
Describe steps you took — focus on your individual contributions.
R
Result
Quantify outcomes — metrics, impact, recognition, or lessons learned.
